FAQ
Common questions
I have zero work experience. Will this actually work? ⌄
Yes — that's exactly who this is built for. The AI knows how to reframe school activities, sports, volunteering, and hobbies into professional resume content. Your first resume doesn't need work experience. It needs to show you're reliable, a fast learner, and right for the role.
Does this work in my country? ⌄
Yes. The AI automatically adjusts spelling, format, and context based on where you are. Works for students in Canada, the US, the UK, Australia, and anywhere else English is the working language.
What jobs does this work for? ⌄
Any first job — Tim Hortons, McDonald's, Walmart, Cineplex, Shoppers, SportChek, tutoring, babysitting referrals, local shops. Just tell us who you're applying to and we tailor the resume specifically for them.
Can I use it more than once? ⌄
Each $4 purchase generates one resume. If you want to apply to multiple jobs, each one costs $4. Applying to 3 places is still only $12 — less than a Starbucks run.
How is this different from just using ChatGPT? ⌄
ChatGPT is a blank page. You'd need to know exactly what to ask, how to structure a resume, what Canadian employers expect, and how to format the output. ResumeReady handles all of that — it's a purpose-built tool, not a general chatbot. You answer simple questions and get a ready-to-use resume.
