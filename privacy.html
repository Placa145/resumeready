Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ResumeReady ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use ResumeReady at resumeready-five.vercel.app.

Information we collect

When you use the resume builder, you provide:

  • Your name and city
  • Your school name and grade
  • Your email address and phone number (optional)
  • The job you're applying for
  • Your skills, activities, and availability

We also collect standard technical information automatically: IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This is standard for any website.

How we use your information

The information you enter into the builder is used solely to generate your resume. It is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the resume text, and is not stored in any database on our end after your session ends.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except as required to deliver the service (Anthropic's API for generation, Stripe for payment processing, Vercel for hosting).

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your credit card information. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy.

Children and minors

ResumeReady is designed for Grade 9–12 students (approximately ages 14–18). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please have a parent or guardian use the service on your behalf.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information without consent may contact us to request deletion.

Data retention

The information you enter into the builder is not stored after your session ends. Resume content is generated in real time and displayed only in your browser. We do not maintain a database of user resumes.

Your rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of any information
  • Ask questions about this policy

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may set technical cookies required for the site to function.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at resumeready.help@gmail.com. We respond the same day.
